Settings

This prototype stores tasks in your phone’s browser storage. The next version will connect to a dedicated list in your personal Microsoft To Do account.

How recommendations work

  • Tasks are ranked by importance, urgency, due date and whether they fit the time you have.
  • For very short slots, easy tasks are preferred.
  • For longer slots, important tasks that can be meaningfully progressed are preferred.
  • Unclassified tasks are surfaced in the Quick tidy-up card.
